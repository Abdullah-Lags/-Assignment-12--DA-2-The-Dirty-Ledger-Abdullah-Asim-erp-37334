## Objective

The task is to audit the supplied teaching-price ledger, identify data-quality issues, make careful decisions about what to fix or flag, and evaluate suggested cleaning operations. The aim is not to make every unusual value disappear. It is to keep a clear record of what changed and why.

## Dataset

The file contains 97 rows and 6 columns. It describes illustrative prices for four teaching items in two teaching cities over weekly dates from January to March 2026. The dataset itself states:

> ILLUSTRATIVE TEACHING DATA - NOT ACTUAL PBS OBSERVATIONS

These records must not be described as actual Pakistan Bureau of Statistics observations or real market prices.
